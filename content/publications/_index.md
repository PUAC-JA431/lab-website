> **Hsu, F.-H., et al. (2026).** W-doped α-phase molybdenum trioxide with enhanced cycling stability as a cathode material for aqueous aluminum-ion batteries. *Journal of Energy Storage*, 142.  
>  
> 本實驗室運用同步輻射光譜技術揭示摻鎢α-相三氧化鉬的離子儲存機理，顯著提升水性鋁離子電池的循環穩定性。  
> Our laboratory utilizes in-situ/operando X-ray spectroscopies to elucidate the ion storage mechanisms of W-doped α-phase molybdenum trioxide, achieving enhanced cycling stability in aqueous aluminum-ion batteries.  
>  
> ---  
>  
> **Hsu, F.-H., et al. (2024).** The ion behavior and storage mechanism of 2D MoO3 layer structure in an air-stable hydrated eutectic electrolyte for aluminum-ion energy storage. *Journal of Energy Storage*, 84.  
>  
> 研究團隊運用同步輻射操作技術探討2D MoO3層狀結構於空氣穩定水合共晶電解液中的離子行為與儲存機制。  
> The research team employs in-situ/operando X-ray spectroscopies to study ion behavior and storage mechanisms of the 2D MoO3 layered structure in an air-stable hydrated eutectic electrolyte for aluminum-ion energy storage.  
>  
> ---  
>  
> **Hsu, F.-H., et al. (2022).** Correlation of crystal structure and ion storage behavior of MoO3 electrode materials for aluminum-ion energy storage studied with in-situ X-ray spectroscopies. *Nanoscale*, 14, 7502-7515.  
>  
> 本實驗室專精利用同步輻射原位光譜學技術研究MoO3晶體結構與離子儲存行為之相關性，深化對鋁離子儲能機制的理解。  
> Our laboratory specializes in employing in-situ/operando X-ray spectroscopies to correlate MoO3 crystal structure with ion storage behavior, advancing the understanding of aluminum-ion energy storage mechanisms.  
>  
> ---  
>  
> **Lai, C.-C., Hsu, F.-H., et al. (2021).** 1.8 V Aqueous Symmetric Carbon-Based Supercapacitors with Agarose-Bound Activated Carbons in an Acidic Electrolyte. *Nanomaterials*, 11, 1731.  
>  
> 團隊開發了利用瓊脂糖結合活性碳的1.8 V水性對稱碳基超級電容器，運用同步輻射技術探討其電極過程。  
> The team developed 1.8 V aqueous symmetric carbon-based supercapacitors with agarose-bound activated carbons and applied in-situ X-ray spectroscopies to investigate the electrode processes.
