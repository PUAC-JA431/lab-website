        > **關於助理教授 / About the Assistant Professor**  
        > 許峰豪助理教授於國立中興大學材料科學與工程學系取得博士學位，研究聚焦於高性能能源儲存材料的設計、合成與機制解析，並整合同步輻射 operando 技術，用於在實際操作條件下即時追蹤材料結構與反應行為。其研究涵蓋導電高分子、石墨烯、無機氧化物與高熵材料，致力於發展具高效率與長循環穩定性的下一代能源材料。  
        >
        > **Dr. Feng-Hao Hsu earned his Ph.D. from the Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chung Hsing University. His research focuses on the design, synthesis, and mechanistic understanding of high-performance energy storage materials, with the integration of synchrotron operando techniques for real-time tracking of structural changes and reaction behavior under operating conditions. His work spans conducting polymers, graphene, inorganic oxides, and high-entropy materials, with a focus on developing next-generation energy materials with high efficiency and long-term cycling stability.**
