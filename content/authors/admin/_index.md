            > **About the Assistant Professor**  
            > Dr. Feng-Hao Hsu earned his Ph.D. from the Department of Materials Science and Engineering, National Chung Hsing University. His research focuses on the development of advanced energy materials and the integration of synchrotron operando techniques for real-time monitoring and control of material properties under working conditions.
